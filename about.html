Our Story & Heritage

Bringing authentic, traditional Pakistani home cooking back to busy city life.

Behind Our Kitchen

Founded in Lahore, Saffron & Spice was born out of a desire to preserve traditional family cooking in an era of fast food and commercial kitchens. Every recipe we prepare has been passed down through generations, utilizing slow-cooking methods that allow genuine spices to infuse naturally.

We work exclusively with small-scale local vendors to acquire fresh produce, daily-slaughtered meat, and cold-pressed oils. We never use artificial food colorings, commercial flavor enhancers, or preservatives.

Our Kitchen Philosophy

Farm-Fresh Sourcing

All vegetables, herbs, and meats are hand-selected every morning from local Lahore markets.

Strict Hygiene Standards

Cleanliness is our top priority. Cooking areas are sanitized daily following food safety protocols.

Thermal Express Packing

Insulated meal containers ensure food reaches your table at peak temperature and freshness.

100% Non-Commercial

Small-batch home cooking ensures every box carries the care and warmth of a home meal.
